成都 · CULTURAL ROUTE

Chengdu

Chengdu is not just pandas. It's a tea glass refilled without asking, a mahjong tile snapped down at noon, and a hotpot that argues with you at dinner. Spend one unhurried day here.

Discover Chengdu
BEYOND THE PANDAS

Four Places Chengdu Actually Lives In

Meet the four places first — you'll walk through all of them on the day ahead.

People's Park

The city's living room. Tea, ear-cleaners, and matchmaking corners under century-old trees.

Panda Base

Home to Chengdu's most famous — and least motivated — residents.

Kuanzhai Alley

Three Qing-dynasty lanes of courtyard houses, snack stalls, and street performers.

Sichuan Opera House

Where Bianlian face-changing and fire-spitting acts have played for generations.
